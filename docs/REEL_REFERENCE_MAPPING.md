## Motion observations, separated from design prescriptions

- **Movie opening:** V5 is still on the genre shelf at 1.00; selected Toy Story is displaced/enlarged at 1.10; the detail surface occupies most of screen at 1.30; facts/posters are more complete at 1.60. This supports spatial continuity plus independent loading. It does not prove a shared-element implementation.
- **Episode opening:** V3 stays on the season list at 5.00; expanded card is visible at 5.10; screen-sized by 5.30; statistics placeholders remain through about 5.50 and counts populate around 5.60. Retain layout stability and local loading, not the unsupported social metrics.
- **Assistant results:** V2 leaves the illuminated loading border around 7.20; poster rises through 7.40–7.80; layered stack settles near 8.20. Later samples expose WALL-E, Monsters Inc. and Finding Nemo at one shared focal position. The samples do not show an observable finger or prove exact swipe thresholds; gestures in Reel are an explicitly designed addition.
- **Collection opening:** V4 stays on list grid through 5.20; selected surface appears expanded at 5.30–5.50 and settles by 5.70. The useful idea is preserving collection identity; the reference's blur and public like badges are unnecessary.
- **Calendar entry:** V1 has underlying show view at 1.20; new surface rises at 1.30–1.50; by 1.60 its controls and cards are stable. Upcoming content is seen at 6.49 onward. No separate loading sequence or reminder scheduling is established by these samples.
- **Shelf switching:** V6 menu remains visible at 1.50; shelf title changes and empty poster frames appear near 1.60; content fades in around 1.70–1.90. Preserve heading/control position; do not replay entrance animations on every scroll.
- **Stats:** V7 shows scrolling between summary, year controls, heatmap and chart sections; selected year changes from 2026 to 2017. No reliable numeric count-up or chart morph timing was established. Proposed chart animation is not attributed to this video.
- **Press, haptics and gesture velocity:** not verifiable from supplied recordings. Design-system values are independent choices.

## Style curation decisions

Take poster hierarchy, compact status information, cast rails, chronological grouping and selected-content continuity. Keep Reel's warm dark/ivory surfaces, coral signal, original Lumi and private-library identity. Use a single calm typography system. Do not import iOS Dynamic Island, glass tab capsules, floating detached search navigation, rainbow voice aura, neon green ratings, service logos or social controls.

S1's purple/pink social world and S2/S4's bright promotional backgrounds are intentionally not palette authorities. S3's film-first framing and V1–V7's information grouping are stronger authorities for this product. The recommendation stage is Reel's focal signature; it must remain subordinate to readable reasons and user control.
